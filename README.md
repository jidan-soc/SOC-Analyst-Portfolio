## Investigation Methodology

Where applicable, projects will follow a structured investigation process:

1. **Identify** — Understand the alert, event, or security problem.
2. **Collect** — Gather relevant logs, evidence, indicators, and context.
3. **Analyze** — Examine the available evidence and identify suspicious activity.
4. **Investigate** — Correlate findings and determine the likely attack activity.
5. **Map** — Where appropriate, map observed behavior to MITRE ATT&CK techniques.
6. **Respond** — Document recommended containment and response actions.
7. **Document** — Record findings, evidence, conclusions, and lessons learned.
